Two-layer QGIS export: three_feeder_nodes_edges.gpkg
nodes: one bus per feature; electrical phase numbers in nodes attribute.
Loads, PV, capacitors and source equipment are attached to their bus.
edges: lines, switches and transformer connections, distinguished by edge_type.
Protection, controls, meters and monitors are attached to their referenced equipment.
equipment_json contains full definitions; searchable columns contain equipment
names, counts, load kW/kvar, PV ratings, capacitor kvar, and edge electrical data.
Transformer multiwinding connections share the same asset identifier and equipment
attributes if more than one distinct secondary bus exists; do not sum duplicated
nameplate ratings across such branches. Connections are not additional wires.
CRS EPSG:4326; fictitious feeder layout around the Golden geographic anchor.
Original OpenDSS files are unchanged. Source is the existing loadshape-package
GIS export, including the added substation_power monitor.
